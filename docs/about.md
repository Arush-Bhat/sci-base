# About Sci-Base

## What We Do

**Sci-Base** is a curated collection of physics explanations with a focus on deep, intuitive understanding. Rather than reiterating textbook definitions or dense mathematical derivations, we aim to **internalize** the logic and essence behind theoretical physics concepts—and then express them in accessible, imaginative ways.

This includes:
- Using analogies rooted in real-world experience
- Visualizing abstract ideas with mental models
- Breaking down the "why" before the "how"
- Exploring questions that textbooks might not ask

Our topics span a range of complex areas like relativity, quantum field theory, topological physics, and more—all interpreted through a lens of personal exploration and continuous curiosity.

## Who I Am

I'm **Arush**, a B.Sc. Physics student at **Fergusson College**, Pune. I’ve always felt that the real joy of physics lies in *understanding*—in seeing the patterns and principles hidden in the equations. 

Sci-Base is where I consolidate and share the insights I’ve developed through study, thought experiments, discussions, and mental deep-dives into the world of theoretical physics.

If you're someone who wants to understand rather than memorize, you're in the right place.
