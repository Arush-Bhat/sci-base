# Quantum Gravity

Quantum Gravity seeks to merge General Relativity with Quantum Mechanics—a challenge that has resisted physicists for decades.

### 🧠 My Interpretation

To me, quantum gravity feels like building a map for a place we’ve never been. We know the ingredients—quantum uncertainty and curved space—but don’t yet have the recipe.

### 🧩 Major Approaches

- **Loop Quantum Gravity**: Spacetime made of discrete loops—quantized geometry.
- **String Theory**: Everything is made of tiny vibrating strings in higher dimensions.
- **Holography**: The universe might be a projection from lower-dimensional information.
- **Emergent Gravity**: Gravity may arise from entanglement and thermodynamics.

> It's not just about combining theories—it’s about redefining what space, time, and existence *are*.
