# Knot Theory

Knot theory studies how loops and entanglements behave—mathematically. Surprisingly, it may have deep ties to quantum physics.

### 🪢 My Interpretation

I think of knots not as abstract puzzles, but as candidates for the universe’s architecture. What if particles are *knotted* field lines?

### 📘 What You'll Find

- **Topological Invariants**: Numbers that classify how tangled a knot is.
- **Jones Polynomial**: A way to algebraically describe knots.
- **Knots in QFT**: Appear in loop quantum gravity and topological quantum field theories.
- **DNA, Vortices, and Field Lines**: Real-world systems that behave like knotted structures.

> What if the deepest truths of the universe are tied up in literal loops?
