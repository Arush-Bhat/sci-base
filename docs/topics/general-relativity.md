# General Relativity

**General Relativity** redefines gravity as the curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy.

### 🌌 My Interpretation

I see it as a rule of motion written into space itself. Instead of a force pulling objects, it's geometry guiding them. Everything tries to follow the straightest path possible—*but those paths are curved when space is bent*.

### 🌍 Key Concepts

- **Geodesics**: The natural paths objects take in curved spacetime.
- **Time Dilation**: Clocks tick slower near massive objects.
- **Black Holes**: Not just dense matter, but places where spacetime itself caves in.
- **Equivalence Principle**: Free-fall and no gravity are locally indistinguishable.

> General Relativity replaces force with shape, and rewrites how we understand motion at every scale.
