# Welcome to Sci-Base

**Sci-Base** is a personal documentation project aimed at providing intuitive, conceptual, and visual explanations of high-level physics topics—*as I, Arush, have come to understand and interpret them.* 

This isn’t just a summary of textbooks. It’s an attempt to translate the abstractions and mathematics of physics into insight—into ideas that can be **grasped** as much as they can be **calculated**.

Whether you're a fellow student, an independent learner, or simply a curious mind, Sci-Base is here to guide you through the complex beauty of the universe, with clarity, curiosity, and minimal jargon.
